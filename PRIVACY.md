# BlueLyrics Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-07. BlueLyrics is made by BlueGuard Security LLC, Toluca Lake, California.

## What BlueLyrics does with your data

BlueLyrics shows lyrics for what is playing on your Mac. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

- **Music playback information.** With your permission, BlueLyrics reads the title, artist, album, duration and playback position of the current track from the Music app on your Mac. This stays on your Mac.
- **Lyrics lookups.** To find lyrics, BlueLyrics sends the track title, artist name, album name and duration to community lyric services (LRCLIB and NetEase). Nothing else is sent: no identifiers, no account information, no listening history. Lyrics are cached on your Mac so repeat plays need no lookup.
- **Local network sharing.** BlueLyrics runs a small web server on your Mac so other devices on your own network can show the same lyrics. It is reachable only inside your network. It stores no data about the devices that connect.
- **Microphone and speech recognition.** If you turn on captions, audio is processed on your Mac with Apple's on-device speech recognition. No audio leaves your Mac.

## Data we collect

None. BlueGuard Security receives no data from BlueLyrics.
